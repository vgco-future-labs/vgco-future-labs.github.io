About

VGCO Future Labs is a group of independent security researchers working under one roof. Each researcher focuses on different vendor targets within official bug bounty programs — primarily the Microsoft Security Response Center (MSRC) bounty programs — and we share methodology and tooling with each other.

Principles

01 🛡️

User safety first

The safety of the affected vendors' customers always comes before publication, recognition, or reward.

02 🔍

Transparency through coordinated disclosure

We report every finding to the vendor and disclose publicly only after a fix is available, following coordinated vulnerability disclosure.
